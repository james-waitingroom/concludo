# CONTRACT 7: Nordholm Industries — Usage-Based Fees + Rebate

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **May 5, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Nordholm Industries AB (US Operations)** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide: (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Pro Tier), fixed annual seat-based fee; and (b) API/Transaction Processing Services, billed monthly in arrears based on actual usage, as described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing June 1, 2025 and ending May 31, 2028.

### 3. Usage-Based Fees and Volume Rebate
Customer will be billed monthly in arrears for API transactions processed through the Platform at the per-transaction rates in Exhibit A. Additionally, if Customer's **cumulative annual transaction volume exceeds 5,000,000 transactions in any contract year**, Customer will receive a retroactive rebate of $0.002 per transaction for all transactions processed in that contract year, credited against the following month's invoice. Based on Customer's stated projected volume (provided during sales diligence, non-binding) and Customer's industry profile, Kestrel's sales and finance teams estimate a **65% probability** Customer will exceed the rebate threshold in Year 1, increasing to 80% in Years 2–3 as Customer's transaction volume is expected to grow with its own business.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A. Platform subscription fee billed annually in advance. API/Transaction fees billed monthly in arrears based on actual usage.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience; prepaid Platform fees non-refundable.
