# CONTRACT 1: Meridian Health Systems — Standard Platform Subscription

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **March 3, 2025** ("Effective Date") by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Kestrel"), and **Meridian Health Systems, Inc.** ("Customer").

### 1. Subscription Services
Subject to the terms of this Agreement and the accompanying Order Form (Exhibit A), Kestrel will provide Customer access to the Kestrel Platform (Pro Tier) for the Subscription Term.

### 2. Term
The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months, commencing on April 1, 2025 and ending March 31, 2028 ("Initial Term"). Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods.

### 3. Fees and Payment
Customer shall pay Kestrel the fees set forth in Exhibit A. All fees are billed annually in advance, due within thirty (30) days of invoice.

### 4. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice. In the event of termination for convenience by Customer, fees paid for the then-current annual period are **non-refundable**. In the event of termination for Kestrel's uncured material breach, Customer shall receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid, unused fees for the then-current period.

### 5. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. This Agreement, together with Exhibit A, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.
