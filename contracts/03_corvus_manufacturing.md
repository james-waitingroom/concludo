# CONTRACT 3: Corvus Manufacturing — Platform + Non-Distinct Implementation

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **September 9, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Corvus Manufacturing Corp.** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Enterprise Tier) and (b) Custom Integration & Deployment Services as described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing October 1, 2025 and ending September 30, 2028.

### 3. Custom Integration & Deployment Services
Kestrel will design and build a custom integration layer connecting the Platform to Customer's proprietary ERP system, including custom API endpoint development, modification of Platform workflow logic to accommodate Customer's manufacturing approval hierarchy (which deviates materially from Kestrel's standard workflow engine), and custom-built reporting modules not available in the standard Platform. **The Platform is not usable in a manner that provides its intended benefit to Customer until the Custom Integration & Deployment Services are substantially complete**, as Customer's approval workflows cannot function on the standard Platform configuration without these modifications. Estimated duration: 5 months.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A. Custom Integration & Deployment fees billed in three milestone installments. Platform subscription fees begin billing upon "Go-Live," as defined in Exhibit A, which is expected to coincide with substantial completion of the integration services.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience.
