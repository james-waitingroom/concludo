# CONTRACT 2: Brightwell Logistics — Platform + Distinct Implementation

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **June 14, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Brightwell Logistics, LLC** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Enterprise Tier) and (b) implementation services as described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing July 1, 2025 and ending June 30, 2028. Auto-renews for successive 12-month terms absent 60 days' non-renewal notice.

### 3. Implementation Services
Kestrel will provide standard configuration, data field mapping, and administrator training to enable Customer's use of the standard Platform functionality. Implementation services do not involve custom software development, source code modification, or material changes to the Platform's core functionality. Customer may access and use the Platform independent of, and prior to completion of, the implementation services, using default configuration settings.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A. Platform fees billed annually in advance. Implementation fee billed upon contract execution, due within 30 days.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience. Prepaid Platform fees for the then-current period are non-refundable upon Customer-initiated termination for convenience.
