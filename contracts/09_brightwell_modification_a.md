# CONTRACT 9: Brightwell Logistics — Year 2 Seat Expansion (Modification A)

## AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of **July 8, 2026** and modifies the Master Subscription Agreement dated June 14, 2025 (the "Original Agreement") between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Brightwell Logistics, LLC** ("Customer"). This Amendment does not modify any other term of the Original Agreement.

### Background
Customer has requested additional Platform seats to support headcount growth in its operations division. The Original Agreement's Initial Term (through June 30, 2028) and all other Platform Subscription terms remain unchanged and continue to apply to the seats licensed under the Original Agreement.

### 1. Additional Seats
Effective August 1, 2026, Kestrel will provide Customer an additional **150 seats** of Platform Subscription (Enterprise Tier), at the same **$600/seat/year** rate as the Original Agreement's Enterprise Tier seats — consistent with Kestrel's then-current standard list price for Enterprise Tier seats at this volume tier, and consistent with pricing offered to other customers purchasing comparable seat volumes on a standalone basis during the same period. No discount was applied to this addition relative to standard pricing.

### 2. Term of Additional Seats
The additional seats will be co-terminus with the remaining Original Agreement term (August 1, 2026 through June 30, 2028 — approximately 23 months).

### 3. Fees
Additional annual fee for the 150 seats: $90,000/year (full annual rate; prorated for the partial first period per Section 4 below).

### 4. Billing
Prorated fee for Aug 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027 (11 months): $82,500, due upon execution of this Amendment. Full annual fee ($90,000) due at each subsequent anniversary of this Amendment's effective date through the remaining term.

**No other terms of the Original Agreement, including implementation services already completed under the Original Agreement, are affected by this Amendment.**
