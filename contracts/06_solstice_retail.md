# CONTRACT 6: Solstice Retail Group — Bundled Discount Requiring Residual Approach

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **August 20, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Solstice Retail Group, Inc.** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide: (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Enterprise Tier); (b) Implementation Services; and (c) the "Advanced Analytics" add-on module, all as described in Exhibit A, bundled as a single negotiated package price.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing September 15, 2025 and ending September 14, 2028.

### 3. Nature of Bundled Pricing
The fees set forth in Exhibit A reflect a single negotiated bundle price agreed upon as part of a competitive displacement deal (Customer was migrating from a competitor product). The bundle price reflects an aggregate 22% discount relative to the sum of Kestrel's standard list prices for the three components, individually. Sales leadership approved this discount given the strategic/competitive nature of the win. The Advanced Analytics module has been sold as a standalone add-on to 11 other customers in the past 12 months at consistent list pricing; the Platform subscription has extensive observable comparable pricing; however, no other customer has purchased this specific combination of Platform + Implementation + Advanced Analytics as a single bundled, non-itemized negotiated price, making the Implementation Services component's contribution to the bundle price not independently observable from this transaction alone.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience; prepaid fees non-refundable.
