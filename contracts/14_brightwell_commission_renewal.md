# CONTRACT 14: Brightwell Logistics — Commission with Expected Renewal Extending Amortization Period (ASC 340-40)

**Note:** This test case uses the same underlying contract as Contract 2 (Brightwell Logistics). It isolates a contrasting ASC 340-40 fact pattern from Contract 13 — one where the standard amortization policy (Initial Term only) does **not** apply, and the amortization period must instead extend beyond the Initial Term to capture anticipated renewal periods.

## Relevant Facts (from Contract 2)
- Total Contract Value: $768,000 (3-year term: $720,000 subscription + $48,000 implementation)
- Contract term: July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2028 (36 months), auto-renews for successive 12-month terms
- AE commission: 8% of TCV = $61,440, paid 50% at signing, 50% at 12-month anniversary

## Contrasting Fact Pattern (Specific to This Contract — Reason the Default Policy Does Not Apply)
Unlike the Meridian Health contract, **Kestrel's standard renewal commission rate for this specific customer segment (Enterprise Tier, negotiated under a special channel-partner referral program Brightwell was enrolled through) is only 1% of renewal TCV**, substantially lower than the standard 4% renewal rate and not commensurate with the level of effort or value an AE provides in securing the renewal relative to the substantial 8% new-logo commission paid at signing. Kestrel's sales operations records (comp plan addendum for channel-referral accounts, dated May 2025) confirm this reduced renewal rate applies to all accounts originated through the channel-partner referral program, including Brightwell.

Under ASC 340-40-25-4, when the renewal commission is **not commensurate** with the initial commission (i.e., the initial commission is effectively also compensating the AE for securing future anticipated renewals, not just the initial contract), the amortization period must reflect the **anticipated period of benefit**, which may extend beyond the Initial Term to include expected renewal periods.

## Additional Facts for the Judgment
- Kestrel's historical renewal rate for Enterprise Tier customers of Brightwell's profile (logistics vertical, 400+ seats) is approximately 85% at first renewal and 90% at subsequent renewals, based on Kestrel's cohort renewal data (internal customer analytics, reviewed quarterly by Finance).
- Customer relationship and account history show no indicators (support escalations, usage decline, executive sponsor turnover) suggesting an atypical risk of non-renewal for this specific account as of the contract's inception.
- Kestrel's accounting policy directs use of a reasonable estimate of the total anticipated period of benefit, considering historical renewal patterns for comparable customer cohorts, capped at a reasonable maximum (Kestrel's policy caps estimated benefit periods at the Initial Term plus two renewal terms, absent specific evidence supporting a longer period, to maintain a defensible and conservative estimate).

## Expected Judgment Outcome for This Contract
- Capitalize: **Yes** ($61,440)
- Practical expedient applicable: **No**
- Commensurate renewal commission test: **Fails** — 1% renewal rate is not commensurate with 8% initial rate; amortization period must extend beyond Initial Term
- Amortization period: **Initial Term (36 months) + anticipated renewal periods**, per policy cap of two renewal terms absent contrary evidence = 36 + 12 + 12 = **60 months total**, applying the high historical renewal probability (85%+) as support for including both renewal terms within the policy's capped estimate
- Monthly amortization: $61,440 / 60 = $1,024/month
- Sensitivity note: if only one renewal term (not two) were supportable, amortization period would be 48 months ($61,440/48 = $1,280/month) — a materially different monthly expense; this judgment should be flagged for explicit disclosure given the renewal-probability estimate involved.
