# CONTRACT 10: Corvus Manufacturing — Year 2 Discounted Upsell (Modification B)

## AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of **November 3, 2026** and modifies the Master Subscription Agreement dated September 9, 2025 (the "Original Agreement") between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Corvus Manufacturing Corp.** ("Customer").

### Background
Following successful Go-Live under the Original Agreement, Customer has requested an expansion of Platform seats to a newly-acquired subsidiary facility, along with the "Advanced Analytics" add-on module. As part of a broader account retention and expansion negotiation (Customer had been evaluating a competitor product for the new facility), Kestrel's sales leadership approved significantly discounted pricing for this expansion, below Kestrel's standard list price for comparable standalone purchases, as an incentive to secure the expansion and strengthen the overall account relationship.

### 1. Additional Seats and Module
Effective December 1, 2026, Kestrel will provide Customer: (a) an additional 300 seats of Platform Subscription (Enterprise Tier); and (b) the Advanced Analytics add-on module for the combined seat base of 900 seats (600 original + 300 additional).

### 2. Pricing
- Additional 300 seats: $420/seat/year ($126,000/year) — **Kestrel's standard list price for Enterprise Tier seats at this volume is $650/seat/year; the $420/seat/year rate reflects a 35% discount**, approved specifically to win this expansion against competitive pressure and not reflective of standalone selling price for seats at this volume tier.
- Advanced Analytics module (applied across all 900 seats): $45,000/year flat — **standard list price for this module is $70,000/year for a customer of this seat volume** (per Kestrel's 11 prior comparable standalone sales referenced in the Solstice Retail Group contract); the $45,000 rate reflects a 36% discount, bundled into this retention/expansion negotiation.

### 3. Term
Co-terminus with remaining Original Agreement term (December 1, 2026 through September 30, 2028).

### 4. Fees and Billing
Combined additional annual fee: $171,000/year, prorated for the partial first period, due upon execution.
