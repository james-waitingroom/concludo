# CONTRACT 4: Halden Financial — Platform + Deferred-Start Premium Support

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **January 12, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Halden Financial Group** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide: (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Pro Tier); and (b) Premium Support Services, commencing in Year 2 of the Term as described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing February 1, 2025 and ending January 31, 2028.

### 3. Premium Support Services
Premium Support Services (24/7 dedicated support line, 1-hour SLA response time, dedicated Customer Success Manager) are included in this Agreement and will commence on February 1, 2026 (the start of Year 2 of the Term) and continue through the remainder of the Term. Premium Support was requested by Customer at signing but deferred to Year 2 to align with Customer's internal budget cycle; pricing and scope were fixed and agreed at signing and are not subject to renegotiation prior to commencement.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience; prepaid fees non-refundable.
