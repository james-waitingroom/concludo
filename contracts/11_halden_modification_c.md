# CONTRACT 11: Halden Financial — Year 2 Scope Change (Modification C)

## AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into as of **March 15, 2026** and modifies the Master Subscription Agreement dated January 12, 2025 (the "Original Agreement") between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Halden Financial Group** ("Customer").

### Background
Customer has requested an increase in its Platform seat count under the existing Pro Tier subscription to accommodate a department reorganization. Unlike a typical seat expansion, this change increases the number of users accessing the **same single Platform Subscription performance obligation already being delivered** under the Original Agreement — it does not add a new, separately identifiable service or module, and the additional seats draw on the same underlying Platform access right Customer already has under the Original Agreement, at the same per-seat rate, integrated into the same single stand-ready obligation to provide Platform access over the remainder of the term.

### 1. Seat Increase
Effective April 1, 2026 (partway through Year 2 of the Original Agreement's term), Customer's licensed seat count increases from 300 to 375 seats (an increase of 75 seats), at the same $500/seat/year rate as the Original Agreement.

### 2. Term
The additional seats are not a separate deliverable with their own term; they are folded into the existing, single Platform Subscription performance obligation for the remainder of the Original Agreement's term (through January 31, 2028).

### 3. Fees
Incremental annual fee: 75 seats × $500/seat/year = $37,500/year, prorated for the remainder of Year 2 (10 months remaining: April 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027... *note: Year 2 of Original Agreement runs Feb 1, 2026–Jan 31, 2027, so 10 months remain from this Amendment's effective date*), due upon execution: $31,250. Full incremental annual fee ($37,500) due at the Year 3 anniversary (Feb 1, 2027) for the seats' final year.

### 4. Relationship to Original Agreement
Premium Support (commencing Feb 1, 2026 per the Original Agreement) is unaffected by this Amendment and continues as originally contracted, extending automatically to the increased seat count of 375 at the same $120/seat/year rate.
