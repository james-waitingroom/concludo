# CONTRACT 5: Rivergate Partners — Cold-Start SSP (New Deliverable Type)

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **April 2, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Rivergate Partners LLC** ("Customer").

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide: (a) access to the Kestrel Platform (Enterprise Tier); and (b) the newly-introduced "AI Workflow Copilot" module, described in Exhibit A, which Kestrel has not previously sold as a standalone or bundled line item to any customer prior to this Agreement.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing May 1, 2025 and ending April 30, 2028.

### 3. AI Workflow Copilot Module
The AI Workflow Copilot is a distinct, separately-licensed software module that operates independently of the core Platform's standard workflow engine; Customer can derive substantial standalone benefit from the core Platform without the Copilot module, and the Copilot module is capable of being used on its own with the core Platform (it is not integrated or interdependent in a manner that would make it non-distinct). This is the first commercial sale of this module; it exited private beta the month this Agreement was signed. No standard price list, discount schedule, or prior comparable transaction exists for this module as of the Effective Date.

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A.

### 5. Termination
Standard 90-day termination for convenience; prepaid fees non-refundable.
