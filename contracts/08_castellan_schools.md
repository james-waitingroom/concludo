# CONTRACT 8: Castellan Public Schools — Termination Clause w/ Partial Refund

## MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **October 1, 2025** by and between **Kestrel Systems, Inc.** ("Kestrel") and **Castellan Unified School District** ("Customer"), a public sector entity.

### 1. Services
Kestrel will provide access to the Kestrel Platform (Standard Tier) as described in Exhibit A.

### 2. Term
Initial term: thirty-six (36) months, commencing November 1, 2025 and ending October 31, 2028.

### 3. Termination and Refund Rights
As a public sector entity subject to annual legislative appropriation, Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at the end of any fiscal year (June 30) upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice, in the event Customer's governing body does not appropriate funds sufficient to continue the Agreement in the following fiscal year ("Non-Appropriation Termination"). In the event of a Non-Appropriation Termination, Customer shall receive a **pro-rata refund** of any prepaid Platform subscription fees attributable to the portion of the then-current annual period following the termination effective date. This right is unique to Customer's status as a public sector entity subject to appropriations law and is not Kestrel's standard commercial termination provision. Based on Customer's budget history and current-year appropriation status (already secured for the full first contract year at signing), Kestrel's finance team assesses the probability of Non-Appropriation Termination during the Initial Term as low (estimated 10-15% cumulative probability across the 3-year term, concentrated in later years, based on the District's historical budget renewal pattern and current multi-year budget commitments reviewed during the sales process).

### 4. Fees and Payment
See Exhibit A. Fees billed annually in advance, consistent with Customer's fiscal year (July 1 – June 30); first partial-year invoice is prorated for the Nov 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026 period.
