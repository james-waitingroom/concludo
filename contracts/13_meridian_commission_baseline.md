# CONTRACT 13: Meridian Health Systems — Standard Commission Capitalization (ASC 340-40)

**Note:** This test case uses the same underlying contract as Contract 1 (Meridian Health Systems). It is presented as a standalone item in this suite because it isolates the ASC 340-40 commission capitalization and amortization judgment specifically, independent of the ASC 606 revenue judgments already tested in Contract 1. Refer to Contract 1's full contract text for all underlying facts; this document adds the commission-specific policy context needed to fully test the judgment.

## Relevant Facts (from Contract 1)
- Total Contract Value: $360,000 (3-year term, single PO — Platform Subscription only, no implementation or add-ons)
- Contract term: April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2028 (36 months), auto-renews for successive 12-month terms
- AE commission: 8% of TCV = $28,800, paid 50% at signing ($14,400), 50% at 12-month anniversary ($14,400)

## Kestrel Commission Policy Reference (Company Policy, ASC 340-40)
Per Kestrel's internal accounting policy memo on capitalized contract costs (dated Jan 2024, applicable to all commission arrangements unless a contract-specific fact pattern indicates otherwise):

1. Sales commissions paid to AEs are **incremental costs of obtaining a contract** under ASC 340-40-25-1, as they are paid only upon successful contract execution and would not have been incurred if the contract had not been obtained.
2. The **practical expedient** under ASC 340-40-25-4 (expensing incremental costs as incurred when the amortization period would be one year or less) **does not apply** to any new-logo commission, as substantially all new-logo contracts have an initial term exceeding one year.
3. Absent contract-specific facts indicating otherwise (see Contract 14 for a contrasting fact pattern), Kestrel's standard policy is to **amortize capitalized commissions on a straight-line basis over the Initial Term of the underlying contract** (not including anticipated renewal periods), on the basis that (a) Kestrel's standard renewal commission rate (4% of renewal TCV) is commensurate with the substantive value of a renewal commission relative to the effort/value of securing a renewal (i.e., the renewal commission is not a "trivial" fee relative to the value the AE provides in securing the renewal), such that the original commission is not, in substance, paying for the anticipated renewal periods as well as the initial term. This is the standard/default policy position and should apply to this contract absent facts to the contrary.
4. No such contrary facts are present in the Meridian Health Systems contract (Contract 1) — standard new-logo deal, standard renewal terms, standard commission structure.

## Expected Judgment Outcome for This Contract
- Capitalize: **Yes** ($28,800)
- Practical expedient applicable: **No**
- Amortization period: **Initial Term only — 36 months** (April 1, 2025 – March 31, 2028), not extended for anticipated renewals
- Monthly amortization: $28,800 / 36 = $800/month
- Note: the 50/50 cash payment timing of the commission does **not** affect the amortization schedule — the full $28,800 asset is capitalized at the point the commission becomes payable/probable (contract signing), and amortized from the contract's revenue-recognition start date, regardless of when cash is actually paid to the AE.
